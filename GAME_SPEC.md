# GAME_SPEC.md: Grow a Planet

## 0. Scope of this build

**First full build (this spec's launch scope):**
- **One planet** (the home planet) with the HOI4-style strategic map and camera.
- Planet growth: Earth Mass, Water Mass, Total Size, 5 stages.
- Clickable regions on the sphere with terrain types, each developable on its own.
- Civilization and economy: 6 eras, 6 resources, per-region buildings and upgrades, offline income.
- Saving, full UI, mobile + PC support.

**Deferred to a later phase (do NOT build unless the owner asks):**
- Planet-switch button and any planet other than the home planet (Moon, Mars, rest of the solar system, rockets, space program).
- Military: Army, Navy, Air Force, Space Force, units, defenses, nukes.
- PvP: async raids, live wars, ranked seasons, matchmaking, power rating.
- Prestige ("Big Bang" resets).
- Monetization: gamepasses, developer products.
- Leaderboards, daily rewards, random events (meteors, alien raids, NPC rivals).
- Map modes beyond the default terrain view.

Design systems so deferred content can be added later through config and content, not rewrites (planet definitions, terrain types, eras and buildings are data-driven; player data is versioned and namespaced).

**Cut list if time runs short (in this order):**
1. Sounds and extra effects
2. Tutorial
3. Planet visual extras (clouds, city lights)
4. Region visual polish (animated borders, glow)
5. Per-era building variety (keep at least 2 buildings per era)
6. Mobile layout polish (keep it usable)
7. If performance forces it: reduce region count BEFORE cutting any region feature.

**Never cut:** server-authoritative economy, saving, offline income, planet stages, the 6 eras, real clickable regions on the sphere, rotate/zoom camera.

---

## 1. Primary goal

Create an entertaining, grind-driven strategic simulator built around satisfying growth, a planet you can watch change, and regions you develop one by one.

Central fantasy: **"This was a barren rock a few minutes ago. Now I can see my civilization lighting up its night side from orbit."**

Optimize for enjoyment and repeat play. Build the foundation so the long-term vision (section 8) can be added without rewrites.

---

## 2. Game feel and visual direction

### Free asset sourcing

Follow AGENTS.md section 3 for all asset acquisition. Everything is free: original Blender models first, then verified free Creator Store, CC0/public-domain, eligible CC-BY, and permissively licensed open-source assets. No paid services. Never use non-commercial, no-derivatives, or unknown licenses. Log every asset in `docs/ASSET_MANIFEST.md`.

### Visual direction

Clean, readable, stylized (not photoreal):
- A planet with strong silhouette and clear terrain colors that read at a glance from far away.
- Visible growth: the planet scales, oceans rise, land and atmosphere appear, clouds, city lights at night.
- Each of the 5 stages looks clearly different.
- Regions are readable at every zoom level, with clear hover and selection highlights.
- Consistent UI style based on the Check-UI foundation.

**Camera (HOI4-inspired strategic view):**
- Drag to rotate around the planet, including over the poles with no gimbal lock.
- Smooth zoom between "whole planet in view" and "close to a region".
- Mouse, keyboard, and touch (drag + pinch) support.
- Framing adapts as the planet grows. No clipping.

**Settings:** reduced motion, sound volume, music volume, effects quality.

## 3. Core gameplay loop

Click/tap to generate Growth Points → grow the planet → select a region → build and develop it → earn resources → advance era → unlock new buildings and multipliers → repeat.

**Currencies and resources:**
- **Growth Points:** generated by click/tap and passive income, spent on planet growth and upgrades.
- **Resources:** Population, Food, Materials, Energy, Money, Research.

**Server authority:** all rewards, purchases, builds, era advancement, and offline income are server-authoritative. Clients send intents only (for example "build building X in region N").

**Offline income:** continues while the player is away, is capped (cap in config), and is calculated on rejoin.

**Economy:** put all values in central config. The agent proposes numbers; the owner approves them in phase reports. Build a **debug time-skip / simulation tool** (disabled in release) to approximate and measure pacing, and report measured numbers honestly.

---

## 4. The planet

### Planet growth
- Stats: **Earth Mass, Water Mass, Total Size**.
- Stages: **Barren Rock > Volcanic > Oceanic > Habitable > Mature World**, with thresholds in config.
- The 3D planet visibly scales and changes with growth. Scaling is driven by attributes on one planet model.
- Regions, camera, and UI must scale with the planet.

### Regions (HOI4 provinces equivalent)
- The planet surface is divided into **clickable regions** covering the whole sphere with no gaps, overlaps, or unreachable areas (poles and back side included).
- Region count is set in config. The agent proposes a number and justifies it with measured performance.
- Region IDs are stable as the planet grows, and saved region data stays valid.
- Hover highlights a region. Click selects it and opens its panel. Picking uses a **real raycast** onto the sphere.

### Terrain
- Each region has a terrain type: at least **land, ocean, desert**. The agent proposes additional types in config.
- Terrain changes what a region produces (terrain-to-production table in config, agent proposes, owner approves).
- How terrain is generated, and how Water Mass affects ocean coverage, is proposed by the agent and approved by the owner in the Phase 3 report.

---

## 5. Civilization and economy

**Eras:** Stone Age > Bronze/Iron > Medieval > Industrial > Modern > Space Age.
- Each era unlocks new buildings, a higher income multiplier, and new upgrade trees.
- Space Age is the final era of v1 and unlocks nothing space-related.

**Per region:**
- Own population and buildings. The player develops regions one by one.
- At least 2 buildings per era.
- Production depends on terrain and buildings.

**Planet totals:** the sum of all regions plus planet-wide era multipliers.

**Pacing targets for a new player (playtest targets):**
- First 5 minutes: the planet visibly grows and the first era unlocks.
- First hour: Modern era reached.

Bot-simulated playthroughs may be used to measure pacing, but must be labeled as **simulated**. Pacing is only Verified after the owner plays it.

## 6. UI and audio

Functional interfaces for:
- Loading and first-session tutorial (cut-list item).
- Top bar: resources.
- Left panel: planet stats and stage.
- Region panel: terrain, population, buildings, build buttons.
- Bottom tabs: **Planet, Civilization.**
- Tooltips on everything, notifications for unlocks and stage-ups.
- Settings.

Use the Check-UI foundation consistently. Test small screens, touch targets, scrolling, safe areas, and modal behavior. Mobile-first: phone, tablet, desktop, console layouts.

**UI sourcing:** the UI must be built with free resources from GitHub or the web (see AGENTS.md section 3, "UI sources"): Check-UI first, then free open-source UI libraries and kits, icon packs, and fonts with verified commercial-friendly licenses. No paid UI kits. Every UI source is logged in `docs/ASSET_MANIFEST.md` and summarized in `docs/UI_NOTES.md`. The final UI must still read as one cohesive style, not a mix of unrelated kits.

**Audio (cut-list item):** free music and sound effects with verified commercial-use permission (AGENTS.md section 3). Separate volume controls. Distinct feedback for clicks, growth, stage-ups, builds, and era unlocks.

No dead buttons, fake counters, or unfinished panels in a release build.

## 7. Engineering and performance

- Modular typed Luau with clear server/client/shared boundaries.
- Versioned profiles via ProfileStore. No duplicate session writes. Never overwrite a profile with defaults after a load failure.
- Validate and rate-limit all client requests, including region IDs. Never trust client resource amounts, prices, or ownership.
- Big-number handling and suffix formatting (K, M, B, T, Qa...). The agent chooses the method in Phase 1 and documents it.
- Performance: one planet model, no heavy per-frame loops, throttled UI updates, reused meshes and materials. The region rendering and picking approach is the agent's choice, but it must be justified with measured performance.
- Target smooth desktop play and **at least 30 FPS on a representative mid-range mobile device.** State which devices were actually tested. Studio emulation is not a physical device.
- Support mobile and PC.

## 8. Long-term vision (DEFERRED: design intent only, do NOT build)

Keep the architecture ready for these. Write no code for them.

- **Space expansion:** Moon colony > Mars > Venus/Mercury > Asteroid Belt > Jupiter moons > Saturn > outer system. Each new world is its own mini-planet with its own growth and civilization, plus a global multiplier to the home planet. A **planet-switch button** moves between planets.
- **Military:** Army, Navy, Air Force, Space Force; units by era. Nukes are a late-game unlock, presented in a cartoon, non-graphic style with no real countries, real people, or real tragedies.
- **PvP (grind, then fight other players):** asynchronous raids on saved defense snapshots first, then real-time live wars and ranked seasons. Power Rating-based matchmaking, new-player shields, post-raid shields, capped resource theft that never touches permanent progress, a Peaceful Mode opt-out, and counter relationships between military branches. All battles are simulated server-side. No pay-to-win in combat.
- **Prestige:** "Big Bang" resets with permanent multipliers, at least 3 tiers.
- **Monetization:** gamepasses (2x Growth, Auto-Clicker, VIP, Extra Planet Slot), developer products (time skips, boosts). No pay-to-win, no undisclosed random paid odds.
- **Random events and retention:** meteors, alien raids, NPC rivals, daily rewards, leaderboards.

## 9. Test matrix (verify and report honestly)

- Full first session as a new player: click, grow, build, era-up.
- All 5 planet stages: visuals, stat thresholds, and region scaling.
- Camera at minimum and maximum zoom, at the poles, and at every planet size.
- Region picking accuracy, including back side, poles, and edges.
- Terrain production differences.
- Every era reached by scripted simulation.
- Offline income: below the cap, at the cap, past the cap.
- Rejoin after progression, builds, and stage changes (region data intact).
- Insufficient resources, invalid region IDs, rapid repeated requests.
- Data load failure and server shutdown saving.
- Exploit attempts on build, upgrade, and reward requests.
- All UI flows on mobile and PC layouts.
- Long sessions for memory, instance, and connection leaks.
